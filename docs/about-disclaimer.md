# About / Disclaimer

## About this project

**Understanding EI** is an independent educational documentation project.

Its purpose is to explain publicly available Employment Insurance information in clear, plain language.

## Prominent disclaimer

!!! danger "Unofficial informational resource"
    This website is **not** an official Government of Canada or ESDC website.

    It is provided for **general informational and educational purposes only** and does **not** provide legal advice.

    It does not provide case-specific recommendations, advocacy strategy, or insider guidance.

    Users should rely on official Government of Canada sources and, where appropriate, qualified professional advice for their own situations.

## Editorial principles

- Neutral, public-information tone
- Plain-language explanations
- No unsupported claims
- Clear separation between explanation and official source text
- No government branding mimicry
