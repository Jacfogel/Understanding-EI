# Understanding EI

A plain-language, educational guide to publicly available information about Employment Insurance (EI) in Canada.

!!! warning "Important disclaimer"
    **This is an unofficial educational website.** It is not a Government of Canada or ESDC website.
    This content is for general information only and is **not legal advice**.
    For decisions about your own situation, rely on official Government of Canada sources and, where appropriate, qualified professional advice.

## Purpose of this site

This project explains, in neutral and simple terms, what EI decision-making commonly focuses on and why those topics matter.

- It summarizes concepts found in public materials.
- It is designed to help people understand terminology and structure.
- It does not provide case-specific recommendations.

## How to use this site

1. Start with [Start Here](start-here.md) for scope and boundaries.
2. Read topic pages under **Concepts**.
3. Use **Official Sources** to verify information in primary references.
4. Check the **Glossary** for plain-language definitions.
