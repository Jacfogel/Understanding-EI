# Voluntary Leaving

## Overview

This page gives a neutral, high-level overview of how EI materials frame voluntary leaving from employment.

It does **not** provide legal advice or case-specific guidance.

## Why EI cares about this issue

Public EI rules assess why a person left work and whether statutory conditions are met.

At a high level, official decision-makers may examine:

- The surrounding circumstances at the time of leaving
- Whether alternatives were considered
- How available evidence aligns with legal tests in public sources

## High-level decision flow

```text
Employment ended by worker choice
        ↓
Decision-maker identifies applicable legal test
        ↓
Facts and documents are reviewed
        ↓
Rule is applied to recorded facts
        ↓
Initial decision and notice
```

!!! note "Educational scope"
    This flow is a simplified orientation tool. Real decisions are made by official bodies using full records and applicable law.

## Common evidence categories (illustrative only)

- Statements from the claimant and employer
- Employment records
- Timeline of events
- Other documents submitted during the process
