# Earnings While on Claim

## Overview

Earnings while on claim may affect benefit calculations and reporting requirements.

## Why EI cares about this issue

Public EI materials include rules for how certain earnings are treated.

## High-level decision flow

- Identify payment type and timing
- Determine whether allocation rules apply
- Apply public rules to claim period calculations
