# Reconsideration

## Overview

Reconsideration is a review stage for certain EI decisions.

## Why EI cares about this issue

Public processes include internal review mechanisms before or alongside tribunal routes.

## High-level decision flow

1. Initial decision received
2. Reconsideration requested within required timeline
3. Review decision issued
