# Appeals / Tribunal

## Overview

Some EI decisions may proceed to appeal bodies under public legal frameworks.

## Why EI cares about this issue

Appeal systems provide structured review of decisions according to law and evidence.

## High-level decision flow

- Appeal filed
- Record assembled
- Decision issued by tribunal level
